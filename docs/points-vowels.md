# The vowel points — explained

21 vowel points across 16 languages, in plain language. Companion to the interactive chart at
[`docs/viz/vowels.html`](viz/vowels.html). For the **label / notation** proposal and its review,
see [`vowel-notation.md`](vowel-notation.md) — that document is about *how to write* these points;
this one is about *what they sound like*.

## How to read a vowel point

A **point** is three facts about the tongue and lips:

- **Height** — how close the tongue is to the roof of the mouth: high (`i`, `u`) → low (`a`).
- **Backness** — how far forward or back the tongue bunches: front (`i`) → central (`ə`) → back (`u`).
- **Round** — are the lips rounded (`u`) or spread (`i`).

The vowel chart mirrors a side view of the mouth: high sounds at the top, low at the bottom,
front on the left. Rounded vowels sit just right of their unrounded twin. Length and nasality
are *modifiers* on top, not separate points.

> Example words are ear-anchors, not exact equivalences.

| `ʊ` | f**oo**t | A relaxed, slightly lower "oo" | 2 · EN DE |

| IPA | Sounds like | What the mouth does | Languages |

| `ø` | French *peu* / German *schön* | Say "ay" with **rounded** lips | 6 · DE FR HU TR ET KO |

## Resolved / under review

- **`ɐ` — removed.** Near-open central. German `ɐ` was the `-er` r-vocalisation (an allophone
  of coda /r/); Vietnamese `ă` is re-read as short `/a/` (a length pair with `/aː/`). Neither
  is a distinct quality, so `ɐ` is gone (22 → 21 vowels).
- **`æ` — decided merge, deferred.** A witness search (incl. Danish) found **no** language
  that contrasts `/æ/` with a same-length front/central `/a/` — Danish's low-front resolves to
  `/æ/`~back `/ɑ/`, which we already keep. So `/æ/` and `/a/` are the same point; English *cat*
  `[æ]` is a realisation of `/a/`. The collapse is deferred to the Phase-2 low-vowel carving
  (it needs no recovery diacritic), so `æ` still appears above for now.

## The modifiers that touch vowels

| Mark | Feature | Plain meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ː` | long | Hold it longer | Estonian's three lengths; German *Bahn* vs *Bann* |
| `̃` | nasal | Let air out the nose too | French *bon*, Portuguese *ã* |
| `˞` | rhotic | Add "r" colour | American *bird* (ɝ), Mandarin *er* |
