The five operators

Every consonant is a base letter, optionally nudged by an operator. Operators are never a label on their own, so a base+operator pair never splits ambiguously.

Vowels 19 points · 6 one-char cardinals incl. y = ə (schwa)

Six one-char cardinals a e i o u y (y = schwa) plus x back, q lower, c round. Only three two-shift vowels (io eo oa) need a pair.

Vowel pairs (io=ʏ, oa=ʌ) overlap real diphthongs by design — that single-vs-sequence question is resolved in the sequence layer, not here.

Modifiers no tier — each is just another letter

Reading it

t͡ʃ→tq
Italian cielo. Hush affricate — merged with alveolo-palatal t͡ɕ.
ʂ→sq
Mandarin shān. Retroflex folds into the hush — no sign, since sh/x are complementary.
tʲ→tj
Russian тётя. Palatalisation is a plain j, no doubling.
tˤ→tqx
Arabic ṭāʾ. Emphatics ride on the qx combo, like the gutturals.
t͈→tw
Korean fortis. Reuses length — no vowel-length contrast to clash with.
ɛ̃→eqj
French vin. ɛ (e-lower) plus nasal j on the vowel.
