The operators

Five letters are never bases — they are the operators, and each does one job on a consonant and another on a vowel. On the vowels they even mirror each other: c and j are primitive and q = c+j, exactly as q=hush is affricate+palatal on the consonants.

caffricate — tc=t͡sround — ic=y

xdental/velar — tx=θ, kx=xback — ix=ɯ
jpalatal — sj=ʃ, tj=c, nj=ɲlower — ij=ɪ
wlong — tw=tːlong — iw=iː

h is a sixth, part-time operator: aspiration on a consonant (ph=pʰ), nasal on a vowel (ah=ã). Labialisation reuses c (round) on k/g, which have no affricate — kc=kʷ (bare u would collide with the vowel /u/). Stacked operators appear only for genuinely two-feature sounds — tch=t͡sʰ, tqx=tˤ, sjj=sʲ.

Try it

Pick a target, then type notation to see it rendered. IPA is the universal phonemic view; the others decode to a language's own writing — Hungarian letter-for-letter, Mandarin Pinyin segments, English a faithful pronunciation. Mandarin tones: end a syllable with a digit 1–4 (kau1→gāo, kxau3→hǎo; 5/none = neutral). Lazy mode unlocks each language's shortcuts. (Italian and Spanish drop in the same way, later.)
