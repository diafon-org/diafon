The operators

Five letters are never bases — they are the operators, and each does one job on a consonant and another on a vowel. On the vowels they even mirror each other: c and j are primitive and q = c+j, exactly as q=hush is affricate+palatal on the consonants.

h is a sixth, part-time operator: aspiration on a consonant (ph=pʰ), nasal on a vowel (ah=ã). Two stacked operators appear only for genuinely two-feature sounds — tch=t͡sʰ, tqx=tˤ, sjj=sʲ.
