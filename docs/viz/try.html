diaphone — try it

Pick a language, learn its labels, and type in the notation to see it spelled. Each sound is a base letter plus at most one operator; the decoder maps the stream back to the language's own writing.

How it works

Every sound is a base letter (p t k s … and the six vowels a e i o u y) plus at most one operator. Five operators shape the consonants:

c affricate
tc = t͡s
q hush
tq = t͡ʃ
x dental/velar
tx = θ
j palatal
tj = tʲ
w long
tw = tː
