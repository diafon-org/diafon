Which fonts can render the view

Every distinct glyph in the uni view, drawn in the fonts a reader is likely to have. Each cell shows the glyph and its capital, as live text rather than screenshots — what you see is what your machine does. Rows run easiest to hardest.

Coverage flags come from reading each font file's character map; the rendering next to them is the browser's. They can disagree — a font may carry a mark and still place it badly, which is exactly what the eye is for. A · means the font file has no such codepoint — nothing to render, so nothing is drawn. Each IPA value links to what the sound is; a modified point links to its base, and the sub-group heading links to the modifier itself.
